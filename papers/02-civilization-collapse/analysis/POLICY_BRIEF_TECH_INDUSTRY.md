## The Technology Evaluation Matrix

Every technology affects two key variables:

| Variable | Description | Your Impact |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **ΔH₃** | Change in trust/coordination | Design choice |
| **Δλ** | Change in modernization pressure | Deployment choice |

**The formula**:
```
Coordination Score = ΔH₃ - 0.5 × Δλ

Score > 0.3: Deploy aggressively
Score 0.1-0.3: Deploy with monitoring
Score -0.1 to 0.1: Neutral - design matters
Score < -0.1: Deploy with extreme caution
Score < -0.3: Restrict or redesign
```

## The TRUST Framework for Product Design

```
T - Transparency
    Users understand how products work
    Algorithms are explainable
    Data use is clear

R - Reciprocity
    Benefits are shared, not extracted
    Value flows to users, not just company
    Community ownership options

U - Understanding
    Products promote cross-group comprehension
    Multiple perspectives are presented
    Bridging is rewarded

S - Sovereignty
    Users maintain control
    Attention is respected
    No dark patterns

T - Truth
    Products support shared reality
    Facts are distinguished from opinion
    Sources are verified
```
