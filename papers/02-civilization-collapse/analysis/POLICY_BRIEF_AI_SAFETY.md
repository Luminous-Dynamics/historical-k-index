# Policy Brief: AI Safety Through Coordination Physics

**For AI Alignment Researchers and Safety Organizations**

---

## Key Message

**AI alignment is fundamentally a coordination problem.** The goal of AI safety can be reframed as: maintaining civilizational trust (H₃) above the coordination threshold (θ ≈ 0.375) throughout the AI transition.

---

## The Coordination Framing of AI Risk

### Traditional AI Safety Concerns

- Misaligned objectives
- Power-seeking behavior
- Deceptive alignment
- Catastrophic misuse
- Race dynamics

### Coordination Physics Perspective

All of these are **mechanisms by which AI could push H₃ below θ**:

| AI Risk | Coordination Mechanism | Impact on H₃ |
|---------|----------------------|--------------|
| Misaligned AI | Erodes trust in institutions | -0.05 to -0.15 |
| Deepfakes | Destroys shared reality | -0.03 to -0.08 |
| Manipulation | Fragments coordination | -0.08 to -0.15 |
| Economic disruption | Breaks social contracts | -0.03 to -0.05 |
| Information overload | Overwhelms verification | -0.02 to -0.05 |

**The common thread**: AI accelerates λ (modernization pressure) faster than it builds H₃ (trust).

## Law 17: The AI Coordination Paradox

```
Core Insight:
AI amplifies coordination dynamics in BOTH directions.

dH₃/dt_AI = dH₃/dt_natural × (1 + A_positive)
dλ/dt_AI = dλ/dt_natural × (1 + A_negative)

Currently: A_negative ≈ 0.7 > A_positive ≈ 0.3

Result: AI is accelerating us toward the threshold.
```

### Why This Matters for Alignment

Traditional alignment asks: "How do we make AI do what we want?"

Coordination physics asks: "How do we ensure AI maintains our capacity to coordinate?"

**Key insight**: An AI that is individually aligned but coordination-negative is still catastrophically dangerous.

## Implications for AI Safety Research

### 1. Coordination Impact as Alignment Metric

**Current practice**: Align AI to human preferences, values, or instructions.

**Recommended addition**: Measure AI's impact on H₃.

```
Coordination-Aligned AI = AI where:
dH₃/dt_deployment > 0
OR
dH₃/dt_deployment > dλ/dt_deployment × -0.5

An AI that builds trust faster than it accelerates change
is coordination-safe.
```

### 2. RLHF Extensions

**Current**: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

**Proposed**: RLHF + Coordination Feedback (RLHF-C)

Additional training signal:
- Does output increase cross-group understanding?
- Does output maintain shared reality?
- Does output support collective decision-making?
- Does output avoid polarization amplification?

### 3. Constitutional AI Extensions

Add to constitutional principles:

```
COORDINATION PRINCIPLES:
1. I will not generate content that fragments shared reality
2. I will present multiple perspectives on contested issues
3. I will not amplify polarization
4. I will support cross-group understanding
5. I will enhance rather than replace human coordination capacity
```

### 4. Capability Evaluation Extensions

Add coordination impact to capability evaluations:

| Capability | Traditional Metric | Coordination Metric |
|------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Language | Fluency, accuracy | Perspective balance, bridging |
| Persuasion | Effectiveness | Polarization effect |
| Assistance | Task completion | Trust maintenance |
| Information | Accuracy | Reality anchoring |

## Recommendations for AI Labs

### Immediate (Next 6 Months)

1. **Add H₃ monitoring to deployment metrics**
   - Track polarization in AI-assisted conversations
   - Measure cross-group exposure
   - Monitor trust changes in user populations

2. **Incorporate coordination principles in training**
   - Add bridging behavior to RLHF
   - Penalize polarization amplification
   - Reward perspective-taking

3. **Develop coordination safety benchmarks**
   - Test AI for cross-group understanding support
   - Measure reality anchoring
   - Evaluate collective decision-making enhancement

### Short-Term (6-18 Months)

1. **Coordination Impact Assessment before deployment**
   - Formal assessment of expected ΔH₃ and Δλ
   - Mitigation requirements for negative scores
   - Monitoring commitments

2. **Design for H₃ maintenance**
   - Fact-checking integration
   - Source attribution
   - Multiple perspective presentation
   - Uncertainty acknowledgment

3. **Governance pacing**
   - Match deployment speed to governance capacity
   - Incremental rollout with monitoring
   - Automatic throttling on coordination damage

### Medium-Term (18-36 Months)

1. **Industry coordination on safety standards**
   - Shared H₃ measurement protocols
   - Common safety benchmarks
   - Cross-company early warning

2. **Regulatory engagement**
   - Support coordination-focused AI governance
   - Participate in international frameworks
   - Advocate for evidence-based policy

## The Alignment Reframe

### From Individual to Collective Alignment

```
Traditional: Align AI to individual human preferences
Problem: Individuals may have coordination-negative preferences
Solution: Align AI to collective coordination maintenance

Individual alignment + Coordination alignment = Safe AI
```

### From Capability Control to Coordination Maintenance

```
Traditional: Control AI capabilities to prevent misuse
Problem: Capability control is increasingly difficult
Solution: Ensure AI capabilities build coordination capacity

If AI maintains H₃ > θ, many capability risks become manageable.
```

### From Racing to Coordinating

```
Traditional: Race to develop AI faster than competitors
Problem: Racing creates pressure to cut safety corners
Solution: Coordinate on safety, compete on capability within safe bounds

Industry coordination on safety = Higher H₃ = Better outcomes for all
```

## Key Insight

**AI safety is not just about preventing AI from doing bad things. It's about ensuring AI preserves humanity's capacity to coordinate.**

A world with perfectly aligned individual AIs that collectively fragment human coordination is not a safe world.

**The goal**: AI that maintains H₃ > θ throughout the transition.
