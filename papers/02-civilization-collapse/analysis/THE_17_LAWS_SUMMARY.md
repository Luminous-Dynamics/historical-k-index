## The Laws in Detail

### Core Engine (Laws 1-2)

#### Law 1: The Threshold Law
```
Statement: Below θ ≈ 0.375, defection becomes the Nash equilibrium.

Derivation:
θ = C/(B+C) where B = benefit of cooperation, C = cost

Physical meaning: Minimum trust for cooperation to be rational.
```

#### Law 2: The Cascade Law
```
Statement: Below θ, trust decline accelerates quadratically.

Formula:
dH₃/dt = -λ₁(θ - H₃) - λ₂(θ - H₃)²

Physical meaning: Network effects amplify distrust.
```

---

### Network Architecture (Laws 3, 10)

#### Law 3: The Topology Law
```
Statement: Cascade velocity inversely proportional to √redundancy.

Formula:
v_cascade ∝ λ(θ - H₃)² / √R

Key insight: Centralization → triggers collapse
             Redundancy → slows collapse
```

#### Law 10: The Network Law
```
Statement: Clustering slows cascade; centralization triggers it.

Application: Rome (clustered) slow, Soviet (centralized) fast.
```

```
H₃ → H₁ → H₂ → H₄/H₅ → H₆/H₇

Reason: H₃ connects all other harmonies.
```

#### Law 8: The Dark Trust Law

#### Law 9: The Feedback Law

```
Statement: Trust is self-reinforcing; distrust is self-reinforcing.

Result: Two stable equilibria (high trust, low trust).
Threshold θ separates the basins of attraction.
```

#### Law 11: The Correlation Law
```
Statement: Harmony dimensions correlate above θ, decorrelate below.

Physical meaning: Coordination coherence breaks down at threshold.
```

## The Deep Truths

### 1. Cooperation is Unnatural
Natural selection produces defection. Cooperation requires cultural evolution to override biological defaults. Civilization is a constant struggle against entropy.

### 2. Trust is Everything
H₃ connects all other dimensions. When trust fails, everything fails. Building trust is the primary task of any civilization that wants to survive.

### 3. The Threshold is Universal
θ ≈ 0.375 emerges from game theory, information theory, thermodynamics, and evolutionary biology. It's not arbitrary—it's physics.

### 4. Collapse is a Phase Transition
Not gradual decline but qualitative change. Same mathematics as ferromagnets. Same critical exponents. Same early warning signals.

### 5. We Can't See It Coming
Metacognitive failure precedes collapse. The civilization loses the ability to model itself. External observers see what internal actors cannot.

### 6. AI is the Final Test
AI amplifies whatever coordination capacity we already have. High trust + AI = enhancement. Low trust + AI = accelerated collapse. We are currently in the middle zone.

### 7. The Cosmos Watches
Advanced civilizations understand coordination physics. They maintain silence to protect pre-threshold civilizations from technology transfer that would accelerate collapse. The silence is compassion, not absence.
