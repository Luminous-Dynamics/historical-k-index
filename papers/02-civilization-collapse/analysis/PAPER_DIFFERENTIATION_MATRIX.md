## Publication Strategy

### Optimal Order

1. **Paper 2 first**: Establishes empirical credibility
   - "We observe θ ≈ 0.375 across historical cases"

2. **Paper 2C second**: Explains the dynamics
   - "Here's the unified theory explaining WHY"
   - Phase transition, information theory, Kardashev
   - Builds on accepted empirical foundation

3. **Paper 2B third**: Reveals deep structure
   - "The threshold isn't arbitrary—it's 1/φ²"

### Why This Order?

- Paper 2: Establishes credibility with skeptical reviewers
- Paper 2C: Provides the "physics" before the "magic number"
- Paper 2B: Delivers the "aha moment" when readers are ready
