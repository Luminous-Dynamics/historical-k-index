## The Seven Harmonies

The K-Index aggregates seven latent constructs ("harmonies") that together constitute civilizational coordination capacity. Each harmony has:
- **Operational Name**: The scientific/measurable name used in text and equations
- **Aspirational Name**: The theoretical construct it operationalizes (in parentheses)
- **Climate Application**: Specific role in climate coordination context

### H₁: Institutional Coherence
**Aspirational Construct**: "Resonant Coherence"
**Measurement**: Governance quality, democratic participation, rule of law
**Climate Function**: International agreements, enforcement mechanisms, policy coherence
**Key Proxies**: V-Dem indices, World Governance Indicators

### H₂: Systemic Interdependence
**Aspirational Construct**: "Universal Interconnectedness"
**Measurement**: Trade integration, communication density, migration flows
**Climate Function**: Carbon markets, green finance flows, supply chain coordination
**Key Proxies**: KOF Globalization Index, trade-to-GDP ratio

### H₃: Cooperative Reciprocity
**Aspirational Construct**: "Sacred Reciprocity"
**Measurement**: Development aid, international cooperation, burden-sharing
**Climate Function**: Climate finance, loss-and-damage mechanisms, trust in compliance
**Key Proxies**: ODA as % of GNI, bilateral cooperation indices

### H₄: Adaptive Diversity & Inclusion
**Aspirational Construct**: "Pan-Sentient Flourishing"
**Measurement**: Diversity indices, minority rights, representation
**Climate Function**: Just transition, inclusive governance, vulnerable population support
**Key Proxies**: Gender equality indices, indigenous rights measures

### H₅: Epistemic Capacity
**Aspirational Construct**: "Integral Wisdom"
**Measurement**: Education access, R&D investment, knowledge infrastructure
**Climate Function**: Climate science dissemination, solutions sharing, technical capacity
**Key Proxies**: UNESCO education statistics, R&D spending

### H₆: Biophysical Wellbeing
**Aspirational Construct**: "Human Flourishing"
**Measurement**: Life expectancy, health outcomes, nutrition security
**Climate Function**: Adaptation capacity, human development, resilience
**Key Proxies**: Human Development Index components, health indicators

### H₇: Techno-Social Complexity
**Aspirational Construct**: "Evolutionary Progression"
**Measurement**: Urbanization patterns, technological density, infrastructure
**Climate Function**: Energy systems, transport networks, adaptation infrastructure
**Key Proxies**: Urban population %, ICT indicators, energy grid metrics

## Usage Guidelines

### In Mathematical Notation
Always use: H₁, H₂, H₃, H₄, H₅, H₆, H₇

### In Text (First Mention)
Use the full form: "Institutional Coherence (H₁)"

### In Tables
Use the operational name: "Institutional Coherence" or abbreviated "H₁"

### In Climate-Specific Context
May use simplified labels in context tables:
- H₁: Governance
- H₂: Economic
- H₃: Trust (but clarify as "Cooperative Reciprocity measuring trust")
- H₄: Inclusion
- H₅: Knowledge
- H₆: Wellbeing
- H₇: Infrastructure
